Development services

Fixed-price work that gets a project ready to pitch, fund and shoot.

Pitch Deck

For writers, directors and producers getting ready to pitch investors, financiers or talent.

A deck written and designed by a producer, not just a designer. We shape the logline, synopsis, tone and comparable titles, characters, look and feel, why now, the team, and the budget and financing ask into a deck that reads in minutes and answers what investors ask first.

See a sample: the Walkers pitch deck (PDF, 2.8 MB). More available on request.

From $1,500. Final quote depends on scope.

Ask about Pitch Deck

  • Story and positioning pass
  • Deck writing
  • Visual design and look book
  • Budget and financing summary
  • Two rounds of revisions

Ripomatic

For projects that need to show their tone before there’s footage.

A short tone reel cut from existing films, music and references, so financiers feel the movie in about two minutes. Built for private pitch meetings, not public release.

From $750. Final quote depends on scope.

Ask about Ripomatic

  • Tone and reference research
  • Edit, music and titles
  • One round of revisions

Sizzle Reel

For projects with footage, a proof of concept, or a subject to put on camera.

A pitch reel cut from your footage, or shot new, that sells the project’s world, its people and why it should exist.

Example: the pitch teaser for Dancing Art Dealer.

From $1,500 to edit your footage. Shooting quoted separately.

Ask about Sizzle Reel

  • Story structure and script for the reel
  • Edit, color and sound
  • Titles and graphics
  • Two rounds of revisions

Budget and Production Plan

For projects that need to show they can actually be made.

A realistic top-sheet budget, schedule and production plan for a micro-budget shoot: the answer to the first question every serious investor asks.

From $750. Final quote depends on scope.

Ask about a Budget and Plan

  • Top-sheet budget
  • Shooting schedule
  • Production plan and crew outline
  • A call to walk through it

Script Consultation

For writers who want a producer’s read before they pitch or shoot.

Notes on story, structure and character, with an eye on what the script will cost to make, so you can tighten the story and the budget at the same time.

Priced by project. Ask us for a quote.

Ask about Script Consultation

  • Full read and written notes
  • Structure and character notes
  • Budget-aware notes on scope
  • A call to talk it through
