Privacy

What we collect through this site, and what we do with it.

Effective [launch date]

This policy covers prismentmt.com, run by Prism Entertainment LLC in Los Angeles, California.

What we collect

  • Mailing list: your email address, if you join our list.
  • Project inquiries: the details you enter in the Start a project form, such as your name, email, company, project type, timing and budget range.
  • Basic technical data: the services below may log information like your browser type and IP address when you use them.

How we use it

We use your details to reply to your inquiry and, if you joined the list, to send occasional updates about our work. We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't share it with anyone except the services that help us run the site.

Services we use

  • beehiiv runs our mailing list.
  • HubSpot receives project inquiries.
  • Vimeo and YouTube play our videos. They load only when you press play.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this site.

Each of these has its own privacy policy covering the data it handles.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from the mailing list with the link in any email. To see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, email teddy@prismentmt.com and we'll take care of it.

Children

This site isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change how we handle your information, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
