Who we are

Teddy Hawke

Founder and independent film producer

Teddy founded Prism Entertainment in Los Angeles to make ambitious films on budgets that actually exist. He studied film and philosophy at Pepperdine and worked in tech marketing, including at Fizz, before turning to producing.

He runs Prism on a simple rule: keep making things. Each year the company sets out to make a feature, a documentary and a short, and every project is shaped from the start around what it can realistically raise, schedule and shoot. When something changes on the way, and it always does, the job is to find the creative answer first and the expensive one last.

That same discipline now goes into commercial work. Artists, agencies and brands get a producer who treats the budget and the date as part of the brief, and who has spent years making every dollar show up on screen.

Prism draws on a wide network of directors, cinematographers, editors, crew and artists, each with their own style and way of working. Whatever the project, we build the team around it, so the people making it are the right fit for what it needs to be.

Recent collaborators include directors Christina Woo, Kenneth Vargas and Mason Campbell, and cinematographer Karl-Erik Mills.
