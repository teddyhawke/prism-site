Resourceful by design.

Prism is a Los Angeles production company that makes ambitious work fit real budgets, for brands, artists, agencies and filmmakers.

How we work

The budget is part of the brief.

We treat the number and the date as fixed points and design the work to make the most of them, instead of finding out on set what doesn't fit.

Plan it before we shoot it.

Schedules, locations, crew and costs are worked out before anything is booked, so shoot days are spent shooting.

Figure it out.

Something always changes. When it does, we look for the creative answer first and the expensive one last.

For brands, artists and agencies

The budget you approve is the budget you spend, and the delivery date holds. A producer runs every shoot day, and you get finished work formatted for everywhere it will run.

For filmmakers

We develop contained, micro-budget stories with writers and directors: projects shaped around what can really be raised, shot and finished, from proof-of-concept shorts to features.
